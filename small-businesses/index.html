Common Misconceptions

"We're too small to be a target"

Small businesses are actually targeted more often than large ones, because attackers know they usually have weaker defences. You don't need to be a big name to lose money, data, or customer trust.

"We outsource our IT"

Having an IT provider doesn't mean your security is covered. Most IT support focuses on keeping things running — not on protecting you from threats. It's worth knowing what's actually included and what gaps remain.

"We're not really digital"

If you use email, process payments, or store customer details, you're more digital than you think. A single compromised inbox or leaked spreadsheet can cause real harm to your business and your customers.

"We'll deal with it later"

The longer you wait, the more you're leaving to chance. A few simple steps now can prevent the kind of problems that are far more expensive — and stressful — to fix after the fact.
